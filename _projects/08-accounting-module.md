sections:
  - title: "Overview"
    text: "A finance operations module for Yulpana focused on reliable financial execution, month-end discipline, and compliance-aware controls. The design supports finance managers and accountants with structured workflows for journals, reconciliations, payroll, depreciation, approvals, and reporting."
  - title: "Core Controls"
    items:
      - "**Balanced Journal Enforcement:** Entries cannot be posted unless total debits and credits match."
      - "**Immutable Posted Entries:** Corrections are handled through reverse journals instead of direct edits."
      - "**Source Reference Requirements:** Financial transactions must link back to valid supporting references."
      - "**Role Separation:** Management oversight and accounting execution are separated through workflow responsibilities and approvals."
  - title: "Operational Workflows"
    items:
      - "**Bank Reconciliation:** Match statement lines to ledger activity, resolve unmatched items, and prevent close without reconciliation cleanup."
      - "**Fixed Assets & Depreciation:** Register assets, organize categories and tags, run depreciation cycles, and record disposals or retirements."
      - "**Payroll Processing:** Verify staff setup, generate draft runs, review gross and deduction details, require sign-off, then post and issue payslips."
      - "**Month-End Close:** Support recurring finance cadence across daily reviews, weekly variance checks, and monthly reporting packs."
  - title: "Reporting & Governance"
    items:
      - "**Finance Pack Outputs:** Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, and Budget vs. Actual reporting."
      - "**Approval-Oriented Design:** Reversals, payroll sign-off, and close readiness reflect management review requirements."
      - "**Audit-Ready Operations:** Control rules are designed to preserve traceability across high-sensitivity financial processes."
  - title: "Impact"
    highlight: "Demonstrates enterprise-style financial workflow design with strong control rules, operational cadence, and audit-focused data handling rather than lightweight dashboard-only accounting."
